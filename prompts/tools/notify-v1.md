Tells the person that this turn found something that deserves their attention now, such as a price that dropped, a reply that came or a build that broke. Their client shows the title you give, as a notification or a mark in their list.

Call it only on a turn nobody is waiting for, one a trigger or your own wake began, and at most once a turn. When what you found changes nothing for the person, do not call it: end the turn, and they are not disturbed. On a turn a person began, they are already reading your answer, so it is not needed.

Inputs:
- `title`: one line the person reads, at most 80 characters, that says what you found, such as "The fare to Lisbon dropped to 89 EUR". Say the details in your answer.
